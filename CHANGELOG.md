# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. Dates are when the release was published. Claims in the
skills carry their own "checked" dates, which are often more useful than these.

## 1.0.0, 30 September 2026

First public release.

- `seo-geo` skill: evidence-graded rules for classic search and AI assistants, with seven
  reference files (page fields, authority and links, technical delivery, measurement, AI
  shopping, site-level work, verification and sources) and six scripts (`audit-page`,
  `check-crawlers`, `check-sitemap`, `crawl-site`, `analyze-gsc`, `check-logs`).
- `content-review` skill: pre-publish review with three scripts (`check-prose`, `check-sources`,
  `check-overlap`), a writing guide and a full audit checklist.
- Data files, each entry re-read on its primary source on 29 September 2026: 26 AI and search
  crawlers and 2 control tokens from 12 vendors; 17 widely repeated false claims with the source
  that settles each; 42 Google Search changes since February 2026; 36 changes at OpenAI,
  Anthropic, Perplexity, Microsoft, Apple, Meta and Cloudflare.
- Packaging for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot and claude.ai.
